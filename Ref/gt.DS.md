## c3
#### 4
अथ हास्य वेदमुपशृण्वतस्त्रपुजतुभ्यां 

Now if he listens intentionally to (a recitation of) the Veda, with (molten) tin or lac 

श्रोत्रप्रतिपूरणमुदाहरणे जिह्वाच्छेदो 

his ears shall be filled. If he recites (Vedic texts), his tongue shall be cut out.

धारणे शरीरभेदः

If he remembers them, his body shall be split in twain.
