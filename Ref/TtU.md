## 2.2
#### 1
अन्नाद्वै प्रजाः प्रजायन्ते । याः काश्च पृथिवीं श्रिताः । अथो अन्नेनैव जीवन्ति । अथैनदपि यन्त्यन्ततः । अन्नं हि भूतानां ज्येष्ठम् । तस्मात् सर्वौषधमुच्यते । सर्वं वै तेऽन्नमाप्नुवन्ति । येऽन्नं ब्रह्मोपासते । अन्नं हि भूतानां ज्येष्ठम् । तस्मात् सर्वौषधमुच्यते । अन्नाद् भूतानि जायन्ते । जातान्यन्नेन वर्धन्ते । अद्यतेऽत्ति च भूतानि । तस्मादन्नं तदुच्यत इति ॥ १ ॥

“From food indeed are (all) creatures born, whatever(creatures) dwell on earth; by food, again, surely they live; then again to the food they go at the end. Food, surely, is of beings the eldest; thence it is called the medicament of all. All food, verily, they obtain, who food as Brahman regard; for, food is the eldest of beings, and thence it is called the medicament of all. From food are beings born; when born, by food they grow. It is fed upon, and it feeds on beings; thence food it is called.”
#### 2
तस्माद्वा एतस्मादन्नरसमयात् । अन्योऽन्तर आत्मा प्राणमयः । तेनैष पूर्णः ॥ २ ॥

Than that, verily,—than this one formed of food-essence,—there is another self within, formed of Prāṇa; by him this one is filled.
## 2.6
#### 1
असन्नेव स भवति 
असदब्रह्मेति वेद चेत् 

'He who knows the Brahman as non-existing, becomes himself non-existing. 

अस्ति ब्रह्मेति चेद्वेद 

He who knows the Brahman as existing, 

सन्तमेनं ततो विदुरिति 

him we know himself as existing.' 

तस्यैष एव शारीर आत्मा 
यः पूर्वस्य 

The embodied Self of this (bliss) is the same as that of the former (understanding).

अथातो ऽनुप्रश्नाः 

Thereupon follow the questions of the pupil:

उताविद्वानमुं लोकं प्रेत्य 
कश्चन गच्छती आहो विद्वानमुं लोकं प्रेत्य कश्चित्समश्नुताउ 

'Does any one who knows not, after he has departed this life, ever go to that world? Or does he who knows, after he has departed, go to that world 1?'

The answer is: 

सो ऽकामयत 

He wished, 

बहु स्यां प्रजायेयेति 

may I be many 1, may I grow forth. 

स तपो ऽतप्यत 

He brooded over himself (like a man performing penance). 

स तपस्तप्त्वा 

After he had thus brooded, 

इदं सर्वमसृजत 

he sent forth (created) all, 

यदिदं किञ्च 

whatever there is. 

तत्सृष्ट्वा 

Having sent forth, 

तदेवानुप्राविशत् 

he entered into it. 

तदनुप्रविश्य 

Having entered it, 

सच्च त्यच्चाभवत् 

he became sat (what is manifest) and tyat (what is not manifest), 

निरुक्तं चानिरुक्तं च 

defined and undefined, 

निलयनं चानिलयनं च 

supported and not supported, 

विज्ञानं चाविज्ञानं च 

(endowed with) knowledge and without knowledge (as stones), 

सत्यं चानृतं च 

real and unreal 2. 

सत्यमभवत् 
यदिदं किञ्च 

The Sattya (true) became all this whatsoever, 

तत्सत्यमित्याचक्षते 

and therefore the wise call it (the Brahman) Sat-tya (the true).

अ तदप्येष श्लोको भवति

## 2.7
#### 1
असद्वा इदमग्र आसीत् 

'In the beginning this was non-existent (not yet defined by form and name). 

ततो वै सदजायत 

From it was born what exists. 

तदात्मानं स्वयमकुरुत 
तस्मात्तत्सुकृतमुच्यत इति 

That made itself its Self, therefore it is called the Self-made 3.' 

यद्वै तत् सुकृतम् 
रसो वै सः 

That which is Self-made is a flavour 4 (can be tasted), 

रसं ह्येवायं लब्ध्वानन्दी भवति 

for only after perceiving a flavour can any one perceive pleasure. 

को ह्येवान्यात्कः प्राण्यात् 

Who could breathe, who could breathe forth, 

यदेष आकाश आनन्दो न स्यात् 

if that bliss (Brahman) existed not in the ether (in the heart)? 

एष ह्येवानन्दयाति 

For he alone causes blessedness.

यदा ह्येवैष एतस्मिन्नदृश्ये ऽनात्म्ये ऽनिरुक्ते ऽनिलयने ऽभयं प्रतिष्ठां विन्दते 

When he finds freedom from fear and rest in that which is invisible, incorporeal, undefined, unsupported, 

अथ सो ऽभयं गतो भवति 

then he has obtained the fearless. 

यदा ह्येवैष एतस्मिन्नुदरमन्तरं कुरुते 

For if he makes but the smallest distinction in it, 

अथ तस्य भयं भवति 

there is fear for him} 1. 

तत्वेव भयं विदुषो ऽमन्वानस्य 

But that fear exists only for one who thinks himself wise 2, (not for the true sage.)
