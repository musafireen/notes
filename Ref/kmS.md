## 1.3
#### 1
धर्मार्थाङ्गविद्याकालाननुपरोधयन्कामसूत्रं तदङ्गविद्याश्च पुरुषो ऽधीयीत।

Man should study the Kama Sutra and the arts and sciences subordinate thereto, in addition to the study of the arts and sciences contained in Dharma and Artha. 
#### 2
प्राग्यौवनात्स्त्री। 

Even young maids should study this Kama Sutra along with its arts and sciences before marriage, 

प्रत्ता च पत्युरभिप्रायात्।

and after it they should continue to do so with the consent of their husbands.
#### 3
योषितां शास्त्रग्रहणस्याभावादनर्थकमिह शास्त्रे स्त्रीशासनमित्याचार्याः।

Here some learned men object, and say that females, not being allowed to study any science, should not study the Kama Sutra.
#### 11
सन्त्यपि खलु शास्त्रप्रहतबुद्धयो गणिका राजपुत्र्यो महामात्रदुहितरश्च

And from experience we find that some women, such as daughters of princes and their ministers, and public women, are actually versed in the Kama Shastra.
#### 13
अभ्यासप्रयोज्यांश्च चातुःषष्टिकान्योगान्कन्या रहस्येकाकिन्यभ्यसेत्

She should study alone in private the sixty-four practices that form a part of the Kama Shastra. 
#### 14
आचार्यास्तु कन्यानां प्रवृत्तपुरुषसंप्रयोगा 
सहसंप्रवृद्धा धात्रेयिका 

Her teacher should be one of the following persons, viz., the daughter of a nurse brought up with her and already married,

तथाभूता वा निरत्ययसंभाषणा सखी 

[12] or a female friend who can be trusted in everything, 

सवयाश्च मातृश्वसा 

or the sister of her mother (i.e., her aunt), 

विस्रब्धा तत्स्थानीया वृद्धदासी 

or an old female servant, 

पूर्वसंसृष्टा वा भिक्षुकी 

or a female beggar who may have formerly lived in the family, 

स्वसा च विश्वासप्रयोगात्

or her own sister, who can always be trusted.

उत्सादने  संवाहने  केशमर्दने  च  कौशलम्  (44),

अक्षरमुष्तिकाकथनम्  (45),

म्लेच्छितविकल्पाः  (46),  

[25]The art of speaking by changing the forms of words. It is of various kinds. Some speak by changing the beginning and end of words, others by adding unnecessary letters between every syllable of a word, and so on.

देश-भाषाविज्ञानम्  (47),

पुष्पशकटिका  (48),

निमित्तज्ञानम्  (49), यंत्र-मातृका  (50),  धारण-मातृका  (51),

संपाठ्यम्  (52),  

Drawing inferences, reasoning or inferring.

मानसी  काव्य-क्रिया  (53),  

Mental exercises, such as completing stanzas or verses on receiving a part of them; or supplying one, two or three lines when the remaining lines are given indiscriminately from different verses, so as to make the whole an entire verse with regard to its meaning; or arranging the words of a verse written irregularly by separating the vowels from the consonants, or leaving them out altogether; or putting into verse or prose sentences represented by signs or symbols. There are many other such exercises.

अभिधानकोशः  (54),

छंदो-ज्ञानम्  (55),

विद्यानां  ज्ञानम् इति  चतुःषष्टिर्  अंगविद्या।   

कामसूत्रावयविंयः।   
#### 16
पाञ्चालिकी च चतुःषष्टिरपरा 
तस्याः प्रयोगानन्ववेत्य सांप्रयोगिके वक्ष्यामः 
कामस्य तदात्मकत्वात्

The following are the arts to be studied, together with the Kama Sutra:—
#### 17
आभिरभ्युच्छ्रिता वेश्या शीलरूपगुणान्विता 

A public woman, endowed with a good disposition, beauty and other winning qualities, and also versed in the above arts, 

लभते गणिकाशब्दं स्थानं च जनसंसदि

obtains the name of a Ganika, or public woman of high quality,[26] and receives a seat of honour in an assemblage of men. 
#### 18
पूजिता सा सदा राज्ञा गुणवद्भिश्च संस्तुता 

She is, moreover, always respected by the king, and praised by learned men, 

प्रार्थनीयाभिगम्या च लक्ष्यभूता च जायते

and her favour being sought for by all, she becomes an object of universal regard. 
#### 19
योगज्ञा राजपुत्री च महामात्रसुता 

The daughter of a king too, as well as the daughter of a minister, being learned in the above arts, 

तथा सहस्रान्तःपुरमपि स्ववशे कुरुते पतिम्

can make their husbands favourable to them, even though these may have thousands of other wives besides themselves. 
#### 20
तथा पतिवियोगे च व्यसनं दारुणं गता 
देशान्तरे ऽपि विद्याभिः सा सुखेनैव जीवति

And in the same manner, if a wife becomes separated from her husband, and falls into distress, she can support herself easily, even in a foreign country, by means of her knowledge of these arts. 
#### 22
कलानां ग्रहणादेव सौभाग्यमुपजायते 
देशकालौ त्वपेक्ष्यासां प्रयोगः संभवेन्न वा

Even the bare knowledge of them gives attractiveness to a woman, though the practice of them may be only possible or otherwise according to the circumstances of each case.

## 4.1
#### 1
भार्यैकचरिणी गूढविश्रम्भा देववत् पतिम् आनुकुल्येन वर्तेत।

A virtuous woman, who has affection for her husband, should act in conformity with his wishes as if he were a divine being, 
#### 2
तन्मतेन कुटुम्बचिन्ताम् आत्मनि संनिवेशयेत्।

and with his consent should take upon herself the whole care of his family. 
#### 3
वेश्म च शुचि सुसंमृष्टस्थानं विरचितविविधकुसुमं 

She should keep the whole house well cleaned, and arrange flowers of various kinds in different parts of it, 

श्लक्ष्णभूमितलं हृद्यदर्शनं  

and make the floor smooth and polished so as to give the whole a neat and becoming appearance. 

त्रिषवणाचरितबलिकर्म

She should surround the house with a garden, and place ready in it all the materials required for the morning, noon and even sacrifices. 

पूजितदेवतायतनं कुर्यात् ।

Moreover she should herself revere the sanctuary of the Household Gods, 
#### 4
न ह्यतो ऽन्यद् गृहस्थानां चित्तग्राहकम् अस्तीति गोनर्दीयः।

for says Gonardiya, "nothing so much attracts the heart of a householder to his wife as a careful observance of the things mentioned above.
