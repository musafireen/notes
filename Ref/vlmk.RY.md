## 5.35
#### 15
विपुल अंसो महाबाहुः 

Rama is a broad shouldered and a long-armed man. 

कम्बु ग्रीवः शुभ आननः । 

He has a shell-like neck. He has a handsome countenance. 

गूढ जत्रुः सुताम्र अक्षो रामो 

He has a hidden collar-bone. He has beautiful red eyes. 

देवि जनैः श्रुतः || ५-३५०१५

O princess! His fame is heard about by people.
##### 16
दुन्दुभि स्वन निर्घोषः 

He has a voice like the sound of a kettle-drum.

स्निग्ध वर्णः प्रतापवान्। 

He has a shining skin. He is full of splendour. 

समः सम विभक्त अन्गो 

He is square-built. His limbs are built symmetrically. 

वर्णम् श्यामम् समाश्रितः || ५-३५-१६

He is endowed with a dark-brown complexion.

## 6.88
#### 23
अद्य गोमायुसंघाश्च श्येनसंघाक्ष लक्ष्मण || ८८-६-२३ 

O Lakshmanal Let a number of Jackals, multitude of hawks 

गृध्राश्च निपतन्तु त्वां गतासुं निहतं मया।

and vultures descend upon you, when struck down by me, you are dead.
#### 24
क्षत्रबन्धुं सदानार्यं रामः परमदुर्मतिः ।। ८८-६-२४
भक्तं भ्रातरमद्यैव त्वां द्रख्यति हतं मया।

The exceedingly evil-minded Rama now itself can see you, a mere kshatriya (military class only for a name sake), ever ignoble and his devoted brother, killed by me.
