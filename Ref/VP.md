# b1
## c1
#### 15
व्यक्तं विष्णुस्तथाव्यक्तं पुरुषः काल एव च। क्रीडतो बालकस्येव चेष्टां तस्य निशामय ।। १८

Viṣṇu being thus discrete and indiscrete substance, spirit, and time, sports like a playful boy, as you shall learn by listening to his frolics[13].

# b3
## c5
#### 1
पराशर उवाच ।  
Parāśara said.

यजुर्वेदतरोः शाखाः सप्तविंशन् महामतिः ।  
From the tree of the Yajurveda, twenty-seven branches, O great-minded one.

वैशम्पायननामासौ व्यासशिष्यश्चकार वै ॥ १ ॥  
They were composed by one named・・・

वैशम्पायननामासौ व्यासशिष्यश्चकार वै ॥ १ ॥  
Vaiśampāyana by name, the disciple of Vyāsa, indeed composed them.
#### 2
शिष्येभ्यः प्रददौ ताश्च जगृहुस्तेऽप्यनुक्रमात् ।  
He gave them to his disciples, and they received them in due order.

याज्ञवल्क्यस्तु तत्राभूद् ब्रह्मरातसुतो द्विजः ॥ २ ॥  
And among them was Yājñavalkya, the twice-born son of Brahmarāta.
#### 3
शिष्यः परधर्मज्ञो गुरुवृत्तिपरः सदा ।  
A disciple, knower of duty toward others, ever devoted to his teacher’s conduct.

ऋषिर्योऽद्य महामेरौः समाजे नागमिष्यति ॥ ३ ॥  
A seer who, even today, would not come into the assembly on great Meru.
#### 4
तस्य वै सप्तरात्रात्तु ब्रह्महत्या भविष्यति ।  
For him, after seven nights, brahma-slaying would occur.

पूर्वमेवं मुनिगणैः समयोऽभूत् कृतो द्विज ॥ ४ ॥  
Thus earlier an agreement had been made by the groups of sages, O twice-born.
#### 5
वैशम्पायन एकस्तु तं व्यतिक्रान्तवांस्तदा ।  
But Vaiśampāyana alone transgressed it at that time.

स्वस्त्रीयं बालकं रोऽथ पदास्पृष्टमघातयत् ॥ ५ ॥  
He struck dead a crying boy who had touched his foot.
#### 6
शिष्यानाह स भोः शिष्य! ब्रह्महत्यापहं व्रतम् ।  
He said to the disciples: “O disciples, the vow that removes brahma-slaying,

चरध्वं मत्कृते सर्वे न विचार्यमिदं तथा ॥ ६ ॥  
Perform it all for my sake; this is not to be questioned.”
#### 7
अथाह याज्ञवल्क्यस्तु किमेभिर्भगवन् द्विजैः ।  
Then Yājñavalkya said: “What use are these, O venerable one, the twice-born,

क्लेशितैरल्पतेजोभिश्वरिष्येऽहमिदं व्रतम् ॥ ७ ॥  
Who are afflicted and of little power? I alone shall perform this vow.”
#### 8
ततः क्रुद्धो गुरुः प्राह याज्ञवल्क्यं महामतिः ।  
Then the great-minded teacher, angered, spoke to Yājñavalkya:

मुच्यतां यत् त्वयाधीतं मत्तो विप्रावमानक ॥ ८ ॥  
“Give up what you have learned from me, O one who insults Brahmins.”
#### 9
निस्तेजसो वदस्येनान् यस्त्वं ब्राह्मणपुङ्गवान् ।  
You speak of them as lacking splendor, though you are foremost among Brahmins.

तेन शिष्येण नार्थोऽस्ति ममाज्ञाभङ्गकारिणा ॥ ९ ॥  
I have no use for a disciple who breaks my command.”
#### 10
याज्ञवल्क्यस्ततः प्राह भर्तृयैतत्ते मयोदितम् ।  
Yājñavalkya then said: “Revered one, this was spoken by me,

ममाप्यलं त्वयाधीतं यन्मया तदिदं द्विज ॥ १० ॥  
And I too have had enough of what I learned from you, O twice-born.”
#### 11
इत्युक्तो रुधिराक्तानि सरूपाणि यजूषि सः ।  
Having said this, he vomited forth the Yajus verses, blood-stained and embodied.

छर्दयित्वा ददौ तस्मै ययौ स स्वेच्छया मुनिः ॥ ११ ॥  
Casting them out, he gave them back and departed at will, the sage.
#### 12
यजूष्यथ विसृष्टानि याज्ञवल्क्येन वै द्विज ।  
Those Yajus verses thus released by Yājñavalkya, O twice-born,

जगृहुस्तित्तिरा भूत्वा तैत्तिरीयास्तु ते ततः ॥ १२ ॥  
Were taken by beings who became partridges; hence they are the Taittirīyas.
#### 13
ब्रह्महत्याव्रतं चीर्णं गुरुणा चोदितैस्तु यैः ।  
The brahma-slaying vow was practiced by those urged by the teacher;

चरकाध्वर्यवस्ते तु चरणान् मुनिसत्तम ॥ १३ ॥  
They became the Caraka Adhvaryus, O best of sages.
#### 14

याज्ञवल्क्योऽपि मैत्रेय! प्राणायामपरायणः ।  
Yājñavalkya too, O Maitreyī, devoted to breath-control,

तुष्टाव प्रयतः सूर्यं यजूष्यभिलषंस्ततः ॥ १४ ॥  
Praised the Sun with effort, desiring Yajus verses.
#### 15
नमः सवित्रे द्वाराय विमुक्तेः सिततेजसे ।  
Salutation to Savitṛ, the gate of liberation, of pure radiance.

ऋग्यजुः सामभूताय त्रयीधामवते नमः ॥ १५ ॥  
Salutation to him who became Ṛg, Yajus, and Sāman, the abode of the threefold lore.
#### 16
नमोऽग्रीषोमभूताय जगतः कारणात्मने ।  
Salutation to him who became Agni and Soma, the causal soul of the world.

भास्कराय परं तेजः सौषुम्नमुरु बिभ्रते ॥ १६ ॥  
To Bhāskara, bearing the supreme, vast, suṣumṇā-like radiance.
#### 17
कलाकाष्ठानिमेषादिकालज्ञानात्मने नमः ।  
Salutation to the knower-self of time—moments, instants, and more.

ध्येयाय विष्णुरूपाय परमाक्षररूपिणे ॥ १७ ॥  
To the one to be meditated upon, of Viṣṇu-form, of supreme imperishable form.
#### 18
बिभर्ति यः सुरगणानाप्याय्येन्दु खरश्मिभिः ।  
Who sustains the hosts of gods, nourishing with moon and sun rays,

सुधामृतेन च पितॄंस्तस्मै तृप्तात्मने नमः ॥ १८ ॥  
And with nectar feeds the ancestors—to that fully contented one, salutation.
#### 19
हिमाम्बुघर्मवृष्टीनां कर्ता हर्ता च यः प्रभुः ।  
Who is the lord, maker and remover of cold, water, heat, and rain,

तस्मै त्रिकालरूपाय नमः सूर्याय वेधसे ॥ १९ ॥  
To that three-time-formed Sun, the ordainer, salutation.
#### 20
यो हन्ति तिमिराण्येको जगतोऽस्य जगत्पतिः ।  
Who alone destroys darkness, the lord of this world,

सत्त्वधामधरो देवो नमस्तस्मै विवस्वते ॥ २० ॥  
The god who bears the realm of being—to that Vivasvat, salutation.
#### 21
सत्कर्मयोग्यो न जनो नैवापः सौचकारणम् ।  
A man is not fit for righteous action, nor are waters causes of purity,

यस्मिन्ननुदिते तस्मै नमो देवाय वेधसे ॥ २१ ॥  
When he has not risen—to that god, the ordainer, salutation.
#### 22

स्पृष्टो यदंशुभिर्लोकः क्रियायोग्योऽभिजायते ।  
When the world is touched by his rays, it becomes fit for action.

पवित्रताकारणाय तस्मै शुद्धात्मने नमः ॥ २२ ॥  
To that pure-souled one, the cause of purification, salutation.
#### 23
नमः सवित्रे सूर्याय भास्कराय विवस्वते ।  
Salutation to Savitṛ, to Sūrya, to Bhāskara, to Vivasvat.

आदित्यायादिभूताय देवादीनां नमो नमः ॥ २३ ॥  
To Āditya, the primal being—salutation again and again among the gods.
#### 24
हिरण्मयं रथं यस्य केतवोऽमृतधायिनः ।  
Whose golden chariot is borne by banner-like rays carrying immortality,

वहन्ति भुवनालोकिचक्षुषं तं नमाम्यहम् ॥ २४ ॥  
Who carry the eye of the worlds—to him I bow.
#### 25
इत्येवमादिभिस्तेन स्तूयमानः स्तवै रविः ।  
Thus praised by him with such hymns,

वाजिरूपधरः प्राह व्रियतामिति वाञ्छितम् ॥ २५ ॥  
The Sun, assuming a horse-form, said: “Choose what you desire.”
#### 26
याज्ञवल्क्यस्तदा प्राह प्रणिपत्य दिवाकरम् ।  
Then Yājñavalkya, bowing to the Sun, said:

यजूषि तानि मे देहि यानि सन्ति न मे गुरौ ॥ २६ ॥  
“Give me those Yajus verses which my teacher does not possess.”
#### 27
एवमुक्तो ददौ तस्मै यजूषि भगवान् रविः ।  
Thus addressed, the blessed Sun gave him the Yajus verses,

अयातयामसंज्ञानि यानि वेत्ति न तद्गुरुः ॥ २७ ॥  
Known as _Ayātayāma_, which his teacher did not know.
#### 28
यजूषि यैरधीतानि तानि विप्रैर्द्विजोत्तम ।  
The Yajus verses studied by those Brahmins, O best of twice-born,

वाजिनस्ते समाख्याताः सूर्याश्वः सोऽभवद्यतः ॥ २८ ॥  
Were called Vājin; hence the Sun became the Horse.
#### 29
शाखाभेदास्तु तेषां वै दश पञ्च च वाजिनाम् ।  
The branch-divisions of those Vājin texts were fifteen.

काण्वाद्यास्तु महाभाग याज्ञवल्क्यप्रवर्तिताः ॥ २९ ॥  
The Kāṇva and others, O fortunate one, were propagated by Yājñavalkya.
