##### 6.24.16
नासतो विद्यते भावो 

That which is untrue doesn’t have an existence. 

नाभावो विद्यते सतः

That which is true has no destruction. 

उभयोरपि दृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः

But those who know the truth realize the ends of both these.
##### 6.24.39
एषा तेऽभिहिता सांख्ये 

You have just been told the wisdom that comes from knowledge of the self. 110 (sāṃkhya)

बुद्धिर्योगे त्विमां शृणु

Now listen to the knowledge about yoga (buddhiyoga). 111 

बुद्ध्या युक्तो यया पार्थ 

When united with this knowledge, O Partha, 

कर्मबन्धं प्रहास्यसि

you will be able to discard the bonds of action

##### 6.25.14
अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि 

Beings are created from food 

पर्जन्यादन्नसंभवः

and food is created from rain clouds. 

यज्ञाद्भवति पर्जन्यो 

Rain clouds are created from sacrifices 

यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः

and sacrifices are created from action

##### 6.34.8
मय्येव मन आधत्स्व 

Establish your mind in me. 444

मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय

Fix your intellect on me. 

निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः

After that, 445 there is no doubt that you will live with me alone. 
##### 6.34.9
अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम्

If you cannot steady your mind and fix it on me, 

अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनंजय

then practise yoga 446 and wish to attain me, O Dhananjaya!
