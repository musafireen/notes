## 1.11-16
### 1.11
पुरा  राजा  मनुर्  नाम  चीर्णवान्  विपुलं  तपः
पुत्रे  राज्यं  समारोप्य  क्षमावान्  रविनंदनः  

In ancient times (Vaivasvata) Manu, the Merciful, the first king of the Solar dynasty, after making over his kingdom to his son, devoted himself to rigid asceticism. 
### 1.12
मलयस्यैकदेशे  तु  सर्वात्मगुणसंयुतः
समदुःखसुखो  वीरः  प्राप्तवान्  योगम् उत्तमम्  

On a summit of the Malaya mountain, the devout austerities of that resolute hero, who was adorned with spiritual knowledge, and whose equilibrium of mind was just the same in adversity as in prosperity, were crowned with the attainment of transcen-dent yoga (that is, union with the Deity.)-10-11.
### 1.13
बभूव  वरदश्  चास्य  वर्षायुतशते  गते
वरं  वृणीष्व  प्रोवाच  प्रीतः  स  कमलासनः  

After a period of a million of years of continued asceticism, Lord Brahma became pleased towards him, and told him to ask for a boon.
### 1.14
एवमुक्तो ऽब्रवीद्  राजा  प्रणंय  स  पितामहम्एकम् एवाहम् इच्छामि  त्वत्तो  वरमनुत्तमम्  

Having been thus addressed (by Brahms), the king, after saluting Him, said: "Lord! I have only one boon to beg of you, which is above all other boons. 
### 1.15

भूतग्रामस्य  सर्वस्य  स्थावरस्य  चरस्य  च
भवेयं  रक्षायालं  प्रलये  समुपस्थिते  

May I have power sufficient for the protection of the whole creation, moveable and immoveable, when the hour of Pralaya will come."
### 1.16
एवमस्त्विति  विश्वात्मा  तत्रैवांतरधीयत
पुष्पवृष्टिः  सुमहती  खात्पपात  सुरार्पिता  

Lord Brahm, the Soul of the Universe, after granting the prayer of the king (in the following words, "Be it so "), disappeared then and there, and the Devas profusely showered a rain of flowers from the ethereal regions.
## 1.17-27
### 1.17
कदाचिदाश्रमे  तस्य  कुर्वतः  पितृतर्पणम्
पपात  पांयोर् उपरि  शफरी  जलसंयुता  

One day, in his hermitage, when the king was making a libation of water to the manes of his deceased ancestors, a carp (a small fish) fell into bis hands along with the water.-16.
### 1.18
दृष्ट्वा  तच्छफरीरूपं  स  दयालुर्महीपतिः
रक्षायाकरोद्यत्नं  स  तस्मिंकरकोदरे  

On seeing that tiny fish, the merciful king, out of compassion, wanted to preserve it and put it into his water jar. 
### 1.19
अहोरात्रेण  चैकेन  षोडशांगुलविस्तृतः

That tiny fish, in course of a day and night, grew measuring sixteen fingers in length, 

सो ऽभवन्मत्स्यरूपेण  पाहि  पाहीति  चाब्रवीत्  

into the form of a large fish, and (feeling uncomfortable inside the water jar where it was placed by the king), cried for deliverance.-17-18.
### 1.20
स  तमादाय  मिके  प्राक्षिपज्जलचारिणम्
तत्रापि  चैकरात्रेण  हस्तत्रयम्  अवर्धत  

The king took it out of the water jar and put it in a large pitcher but there also, in course of a night, it grew three hands in length. 
### 1.21
पुनः  प्राहार्तनादेन  सहस्रकिरात्मजम्
स  मत्स्यः  पाहि  पाहीति  त्वामहं  शरं  गतः  

"I am at your mercy, come to my succour." The king, again hearing these cries of the fish took it out of the pitcher and deposited it in a well. 
### 1.22
ततः  स  कूपे  तं  मत्स्यं  प्राहिणोद्  रविनंदनः
यदा  न  माति  तत्रापि  कूपे  मत्स्यः  सरोवरे  

Later on, the well also proved insufficient. The king then accommodated it in a tank.-19-20.
### 1.23
क्षिप्तो ऽसौ  पृथुतामागात्  पुनर्  योजनसंमिताम्
तत्राप्याह  पुनर्  दीनः  पाहि  पाहि  नृपोत्तम  

In the tank, again, the fish grew a yojana (eight miles) in length, and again appealed to the king, in a plaintive tone, to help it out of the tank. 
### 1.24
ततः  स  मनुना  क्षिप्तो  गंगायामप्यवर्धत
यदा  तदा  समुद्रे  तं  प्राक्षिपन्मेदिनीपतिः  

Then the king put the fish in the Ganges and, finding that it increased there too, he placed it in the ocean. 
### 1.25
यदा  समुद्रमखिलं  व्याप्यासौ  समुपस्थितः
तदा  प्राह  मनुर्भीतः  को ऽपि  त्वमसुरेश्वरः  

The fish went on increasing and increasing in bulk, until it very nearly filled the vast expanse of the great ocean. The king, seeing this, was awe-stricken and said, "Are you the chief of the Asuras? 
### 1.26
अथवा  वासुदेवस्त्वम्  अंय ईदृक्कथं  भवेत्
योजनायुतविंशत्या  कस्य  तुल्यं  भवेद्वपुः  

Or are you Vasudeva; who else has such an extraordinary power to assume such an extraordinary power to assume such a tremendously big forp extending to sixteen hundred miles?"
### 1.27
ज्ञातस्त्वं  मत्स्यरूपेण  मां  खेदयसि  केशव
हृषीकेश  जगन्नाथ  जगद्धाम  नमो ऽस्तु  ते  

I have come to know you, O, Keśava! You are puzzling me in the form of a fish. I bow down to You, O, Hrisikeda, Jagannatha, Jagaddhama." 
## 1.28-34
### 1.28
एवमुक्तः  स  भगवान्  मत्स्यरूपी  जनार्दनः

Being thus addressed, Bhagavana Janardana, in the form of a fish, 

साधु  साध्विति  चोवाच  संयग्ज्ञातस्  त्वयानघ  

complimented him, and said: "O Spotless One, I have been truly known by you. 
### 1.29
अचिरेणैव  कालेन  मेदिनी  मेदिनीपते

In a few days time, O King, the Universe 

भविष्यति  जले  मग्ना  सशैलवनकानना  

shall be deluged with water, along with the mountains and forests. 
### 1.30
नौर् इयं  सर्वदेवानां  निकायेन  विनिर्मिता

The Devas have made this boat 

महाजीवनिकायस्य  रक्षार्थं  महीपते  

to rescue the creation from such a calamity, 
### 1.31
स्वेदांडजोद्भिदो  ये  वै  ये  च  जीवा  जरायुजाः

placing in it svedajas, andajas, udbhijas and jarayujas. 

अस्यां  निधाय  सर्वांस्  तान्  अनाथान्  पाहि  सुव्रत  

O, King! you take charge of this boat and help the distressed at the time of the impending danger. 
### 1.32
युगांतवाताभिहता  यदा  भवति  नौर्  नृप

When you find the boat in danger of being blown away by the strong gusts of wind, 

शृङ्गे ऽस्मिन्मम  राजेंद्र  तदेमां  संयमिष्यसि  

tie it to my horn. 
### 1.33
ततो  लयांते  सर्वस्य  स्थावरस्य  चरस्य  च
प्रजापतिस्त्वं  भविता  जगतः  पृथिवीपते  

By rescuing the afflicted from such an awful misfortune, you will be rendering a great paternal service to the creation. 
### 1.34
एवं  कृतयुगस्यादौ  सर्वज्ञो  धृतिमान्नृपः
मंवंतराधिपश्चापि  देवपूज्यो  भविष्यसि  

And, O, blessed sovereign! You shall reign for one Manvantara, from the beginning of the Kritayuga, and shall be venerated by the Devas."

### 2.1
सूत उवाचएवमुक्तो  मनुस्तेन  पप्रच्छ  मधुसूदनम्
भगवंकियद्भिर्वर्षैर्  भविष्यत्यंतरक्षयः  

Sûta, continuing his narration, said, that, on hearing such words of the Lord, the king begged Him to reveal to him in how many years the time of destruction was likely to come.
### 2.2
सत्त्वानि  च  कथं  नाथ  रक्षिष्ये  मधुसूदन

The king also entreated the Lord to point out to him the means of saving the creation from such a distress, 

त्वया  सह  पुनर्योगः  कथं  वा  भविता  मम  

and to let him know when he would again be fortunate enough to meet Him face to face.-1-2.
### 2.3
मत्स्य उवाच
अद्यप्रभृत्यनावृष्टिर्  भविष्यति  महीतले
यावद्वर्षशतं  साग्रं  दुर्भिक्षम्  अशुभावहम्  

The fish replied that from that day there would be no rain for a hundred years, and the universe would be overtaken by a dire famine, 
### 2.4
ततो ऽल्पसत्त्वक्षयदा  रश्मयः  सप्त  दारुणाः

After that, all the inferior beings of the universe would be scorched to death by the seven ordinary rays of the sun 

सप्तसप्तेर्भविष्यंति  प्रतप्तांगारवर्षिणः  

which shall become seven times more powerful.-3-4.
### 2.5
और्वानलो ऽपि  विकृतिं  गमिष्यति  युगक्षये

In addition to all that, the subteranean fire would shoot-out, 

विषाग्निश्चापि  पातालात्  संकर्षणमुखच्युतः

Sesa, from his abode in the lower regions, would send forth venomous flames from his thousand mouths, 
### 2.6
भवस्यापि  ललाटोत्थस्  तृतीयनयनानलः  

and a furious fire would emerge from the third eye of Siva.-5

त्रिजगन्निर्दहन्  क्षोभं  समेष्यति  महामुनेेवं  दग्धा  मही  सर्वा  यदा  स्याद्भस्मसंनिभा 

Thus the three worlds would be crumbled to ashes by the combined fury of all those various fires. 
### 2.7
आकाशमूष्मा  तप्तं  भविष्यति  परंतप
ततः  सदेवनक्षत्रं  जगद्यास्यति  संक्षयम्  

The sky, with all the stars and planets, would also be destroyed by the heat thus originated. 
### 2.8
संवर्तो  भीमनादश्च  द्रोणश्चंडो  बलाहकः
विद्युत्पताकः  शोणस्तु  सप्तैते  लयवारिदाः  

Then the seven destructive clouds ris. Samvarta, Bhimanada, Dropa, Chanda, Baláhaka, Vidyut pataka and Sónâ, 
### 2.9
अग्निप्रस्वेदसंभूताः  प्लावयिष्यंति  मेदिनीम्
समुद्राः  क्षोभमागत्य  चैकत्वेन  व्यवस्थिताः  

would spring up from the vapours arising out of such a heat, and would rain in torrents till all the seas become united into one great mass. 
### 2.10
एतदेकार्णवं  सर्वं  करिष्यंति  जगत्त्रयम्
वेदनावमिमां  गृह्य  सत्त्वबीजानि  सर्वशः  

In fact the whole earth would be covered with one vast expanse of water, then get hold of that. yonder boat and put the seed of creation and the sacred Vedas in it. 
### 2.11
आरोप्य  रज्जुयोगेन  मत्प्रदत्तेन  सुव्रत
संयंय  नावं  मच्छृङ्गे  मत्स्यभावाभिरक्षितः  

After that, fasten the boat to my horn by means of this rope that I give you, and then the contents of the barge will be saved by my glory." 
### 2.12
एकः  स्थास्यसि  देवेषु  दग्धेष्वपि  परंतप
सोमसूर्यावहं  ब्रह्मा  चतुर्लोकसमंवितः  

O, Pious One! when everything will be destroyed, your good-self, the moon, the sun, myself, Brahma, 
### 2.13
नर्मदा  च  नदी  पुंया  मार्कंडेयो  महाृषिः
भवो  वेदाः  पुराणानि  विद्याभिः  सर्वतोवृतम्  

the sacred river Narmada, the great sage Märkandeya, the sacred Vedas, the Puriņas, the God Siva, the various sciences, will alone be saved, 
### 2.14
त्वया  सार्धमिदं  विश्वं  स्थास्यत्यंतरसंक्षयेेवमेकार्णवे  जाते  चाक्षुषांतरसंक्षये  

and the reign of king Chaksusa Manu shall terminate with the coming partial dissolution.-8-14.
### 2.15
वेदांप्रवर्तयिष्यामि  त्वत्सर्गादौ  महीपतेेवमुक्त्वा  स  भगवांस्  तत्रैवांतरधीयत  

At the beginning of the re-creation of the Universe which would follow the period of destruction, I shall propagate the Vedic knowledge." So saying, He suddenly vanished away.-15.
## 2.16-19
### 2.16
मनुर्  अप्यास्थितो  योगं  वासुदेवप्रसादजम्

The king engaged himself in the practice of Yoga, to whom Lord Vasudeva 

अभ्यसन्  यावद् आभूतसंप्लवं  पूर्वसूचितम्  

had given intimation till the time of dissolution.
### 2.17
काले  यथोक्ते  संजाते वासुदेवमुखोद्गते 

At the commencement of dissolution, as said by Vasudeva's mouth

शृङ्गी  प्रादुर्बभूवाथ  मत्स्यरूपी  जनार्दनः  

the Lord appeared again in the form of horned fish. 
### 2.18
भुजंगो  रज्जुरूपेण  मनो  पार्श्वमुपागमत्

At the same time, Sesa, the Serpent King, appeared before the King Vaivasvata Manu, in the shape of a rope, 

भूतांसर्वांसमाकृष्य  योगेनारोप्य  धर्मवित्  

and the king, through his Yogic power, collected together all living beings and put them in the boat. 
### 2.19
भुजंगरज्ज्वा  मत्स्यस्य  शृङ्गे  नावमयोजयत्उपर्युपस्थितस्तस्याः  प्रिपत्य  जनार्दनम्  

And, after fastening the boat to the horn of the fish, by means of the rope, the king saluted the Lord and got into it
