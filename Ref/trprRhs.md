# c11
## 72
पश्यैन्द्रजालिकं राम निरुपादानयोगतः ।
भासयन्तं जगच्चित्रं सङ्कल्पादेव सर्वतः ॥ ७१ ॥

Look at the divine magician, O Rama, who manifests the grand picture of this universe everywhere, entirely out of His own resolve (will), without the use of any physical materials, 

(may not actually  be 11.72 . it is 11.72 in srinivasa tika)

## 73
साधारणं स्थिरं स्वार्थक्रियार्हं भूय एव तु ।

common to all, stable, and capable of fulfilling purposeful actions; 

स्वात्मन्युपसंहरेच्च जगदेवं विभसते ॥ ७२ ॥

yet, he can dissolve that entire world back into himself. In the exact same way, this universe shines forth.

## 81
केवलं भावनामात्रात् सत्यता जगति स्थिता ।

The reality found in the world exists solely due to intense mental conviction (contemplation). 

शून्यताभावनेनापि शून्यं निष्प्रतिघं भवेत् ॥ ८१ ॥

By deeply meditating upon voidness (nothingness), even the physical world can become a non-obstructive void.
## 82
भावना ह्यप्रमाणत्ववैधुर्येण स्थिरीकृता ।

When an intense feeling (or conviction) is firmly established, even in the absence of valid proof, 

भवेत्तदात्मभावेन सत्यमेतद् महीपते ॥ ८२ ॥

it becomes reality through absorption into its own nature, O King!
