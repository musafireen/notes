# b12
## c8
#### 20
अपि विद्याधरैश्वर्यं वेतालस्य प्रसादतः ।
किं त्रिविक्रमसेनेन न प्राप्तं भूभुजा पुरा ।। २०

Did not king Trivikramasena obtain of old time the sovereignty of the Vidyádharas by the favour of a Vetála? 
#### 21
तथा च कथयाम्येतां तदीया ते कथां शृणु ।

Listen now, I will tell you his story in proof of it.”

प्रतिष्ठानाभिधानोऽस्ति देशो गोदावरीतटे ।। २१

On the banks of the Godávarí there is a place named Pratishṭhána. 
#### 22
तत्र विक्रमसेनस्य पुत्रः शक्रपराक्रमः ।
प्राक्त्रिविक्रमसेनाख्यः ख्यातकीर्तिरभून्नृपः ।। २२

In it there lived of old time a famous king, named Trivikramasena, the son of Vikramasena, equal to Indra in might. 
#### 23
तस्य प्रत्यहमास्थानगतस्योपेत्य भूपतेः ।
सेवार्थं क्षान्तिशीलाख्यो भिक्षुः फलमुपानयत् ।। २३

Every day, when he was in his hall of audience, a mendicant named Kshántiśíla came to him, to pay him his respects, and presented him with a fruit. 
#### 24
सोऽपि राजा तदादाय फलमासन्नवर्तिनः ।
हस्ते ददौ प्रतिदिनं कोषागाराधिकारिणः ।। २४

And every day, the king as soon as he received the fruit, gave it into the hand of the superintendent of his treasury who was near him. 
#### 25
इत्थं गतेषु वर्षेषु दशस्वत्र किलैकदा ।
दत्त्वा राज्ञे फलं तस्मै भिक्षावास्थानतो गते ।। २५

In this way ten years passed, but one day, when the mendicant had left the hall of audience, after giving the fruit to the king, 
#### 26
स राजा तत्फलं प्रादात्प्रविष्टायात्र दैवतः ।
क्रीडामर्कटपोताय हस्तभ्रष्टाय रक्षिणाम् ।। २६

the king gave it to a young pet monkey, that had escaped from the hands of its keepers, and happened to enter there. 
#### 27
स मर्कटस्तदश्नाति यावत्तावत्फलात्ततः ।
विभिन्नमध्यान्निरगादनर्घं रत्नमुत्तमम् ।। २७

While the monkey was eating that fruit, it burst open, and there came out of it a splendid [233]priceless jewel. 
#### 28
तदृष्ट्वादाय पप्रच्छ तं भाण्डागारिकं नृपः ।

When the king saw that, he took up the jewel, and asked the treasurer the following question, 

भिक्षूपनीतानि मया यानि नित्यं फलानि ते ।। २८

"after the fruits were given to me by the mendicant, 
#### 29
हस्ते दत्तानि तानि क्व स्थापितानि सदा त्वया ।

Where have you put them which I have been in the habit of handing over to you” 

तच्छ्रुत्वा तं स सभयः कोषाध्यक्षो व्यजिज्ञपत् ।। २९

When the superintendent of the treasury heard that, he was full of fear, and he said to the king, 
#### 30
क्षिप्तानि तान्यनुद्घाट्य मया गञ्जे गवाक्षतः ।

“I used to throw them into the treasury from the window without opening the door; 

यद्यादिशसि तद्देव तमुद्घाट्य गवेषये ।। ३०

if your Majesty orders me, I will open it and look for them.” 
#### 31
इत्यूचिवाननुमतो राज्ञा गत्वा क्षणेन सः ।
कोषाध्यक्षः समागत्य प्रभुं व्यज्ञापयत्पुनः ।। ३१

When the treasurer said this, the king gave him leave to do so, and he went away, and soon returned, and said to the king, 
#### 32
शीर्णानि नात्र पश्यामि कोषे तानि फलान्यहम् ।
रत्नराशिं तु पश्यामि रश्मिज्वालाकुलं विभो ।। ३२

“I see that those fruits have all rotted away in the treasury, and I also see that there is a heap of jewels there resplendent with radiant gleams.”
#### 33
तच्छ्रुत्वा तान्मणीन्दत्त्वा तुष्टोऽस्मै कोषरक्षिणे ।
राजान्येद्युरपृच्छत्स भिक्षुं तं प्राग्वदागतम् ।। ३३

When the king heard it, he was pleased, and gave those jewels to the treasurer, and the next day he said to the mendicant, who came as before, 
#### 34
भिक्षो धनव्ययेनैवं सेवसे मां किमन्वहम् ।
नेदानीं ते ग्रहीष्यामि फलं यावन्न वक्ष्यसि ।। ३४

“Mendicant, why do you court me every day with great expenditure of wealth? I will not take your fruit to-day until you tell me.” 
#### 35
इत्युक्तवन्तं राजानं भिक्षुस्तं विजनेऽब्रवीत् ।

When the king said this, the mendicant said to him in private, 

वीरसाचिव्यसापेक्षं मन्त्रसाधनमस्ति मे ।। ३५

“I have an incantation to perform which requires the aid of a brave man, 
#### 36
तत्र वीरेन्द्र साहाय्यं क्रियमाणं त्वयार्थये ।

I request, hero, that you will assist me in it.” 

तच्छ्रुत्वा प्रतिपेदे तत्तथेत्यस्य स भूपतिः ।। ३६

When the king heard that, he consented and promised him that he would do so. 
#### 37
ततः स श्रमणस्तुष्टो नृपं पुनरुवाच तम् ।
तर्हि कृष्णचतुर्दश्यामागामिन्यां निशागमे ।। ३७

Then the mendicant was pleased and he went on to say to that king, “Then I shall be waiting for you at night-fall in the approaching black fortnight, 
#### 38
इतो महाश्मशानान्तर्वटस्याधः स्थितस्य मे ।
आगन्तव्यं त्वया देव प्रतिपालयतोऽन्तिकम् ।। ३८

in the great cemetery here, under the shade of a banyan-tree, and you must come to me there. 
#### 39
बाढमेवं करिष्यामीत्युक्ते तेन महीभुजा ।

The king said—“Well! I will do so.” 

स क्षान्तिशीलः श्रमणो हृष्टः स्वनिलयं ययौ ।। ३९

And the mendicant Kshántiśíla returned delighted to his own dwelling.
#### 40
अथ तां स महासत्त्वः प्राप्य कृष्णचतुर्दशीम् ।
प्रार्थनां प्रतिपन्नां तां भिक्षोस्तस्य नृपः स्मरन् ।। ४०

Then the heroic monarch, as soon as he had got into the black fortnight, remembered the request of the mendicant, which he had promised to accomplish for him, 
#### 41
प्रदोषे नीलवसनस्तमालकृतशेखरः ।
निर्ययौ राजधानीतः खड्गपाणिरलक्षितः ।। ४१

and as soon as night came, he enveloped his head in a black cloth, and left the palace unperceived, sword in hand, 
#### 42
ययौ च घोरनिबिडध्वान्तव्रातमलीमसम् ।

and went fearlessly to the cemetery. It was obscured by a dense and terrible pall of darkness, 

चितानलोग्रनयनव्यालादारुणदर्शनम् ।। ४२

and its aspect was rendered awful by the ghastly flames from the burning of the funeral pyres, 
#### 43
असंख्यनरकङ्कालकपालास्थिविशङ्कटम् ।
हृष्यत्संनिहितोत्तालभूतवेतालवेष्टितम् ।। ४३

and it produced horror by the bones, skeletons, and skulls of men that appeared in it. In it were present formidable Bhútas and Vetálas, joyfully engaged in their horrible activity, 
#### 44
भैरवस्यापरं रूपमिव गम्भीरभीषणम् ।
स्फूर्जन्महाशिवारावं श्मशानं तदविह्वलः ।। ४४

and it was alive with the loud yells of jackals,2 so that it seemed like a second mysterious tremendous form of Bhairava. 
#### 45
विचित्य चात्र तं प्राप्य भिक्षुं वटतरोरधः ।
कुर्वाणं मण्डलन्यासमुपसृत्य जगाद सः ।। ४५

And after he had searched about in it, he found that mendicant under a banyan-tree, engaged in making a circle, and he went up to him and said, 
#### 46
एषोऽहमागतो भिक्षो ब्रूहि किं करवाणि ते ।

“Here I am arrived, mendicant; tell me, what can I do for you?”

तच्छ्रुत्वा स नृपं दृष्ट्वा हृष्टो भिक्षुरुवाच तम् ।। ४६

When the mendicant heard that, and saw the king, he was delighted, [234]and said to him—
#### 47
राजन्कृतः प्रसादश्चेत्तदितो दक्षिणामुखम् ।
गत्वा विदूरमेकाकी विद्यते शिंशपातरुः ।। ४७

“King, if I have found favour in your eyes, go alone a long way from here towards the south, and you will find an śinśapá-tree.3 
#### 48
तस्मिन्नुल्लम्बितमृतः कोऽप्येकः पुरुषः स्थितः ।
तमिहानय गत्वा त्वं सानाथ्यं कुरु वीर मे ।। ४८

On it there is a dead man hanging up; go and bring him here; assist me in this matter, hero.” 
#### 49
तच्छ्रुत्वैव तथेत्युक्त्वा स राजा सत्यसंगरः ।
दक्षिणां दिशमालम्ब्य प्रवीरः प्रययौ ततः ।। ४९

As soon as the brave king, who was faithful to his promise, heard this, he said, “I will do so,” and went towards the south. 
#### 50
आत्तदीप्तचितालातलक्षितेन पथात्र सः ।
गत्वा तमसि तं प्राप कथंचिच्छिंशपातरुम् ।। ५०

And after he had gone some way in that direction, along a path revealed by the light of the flaming pyres, he reached with difficulty in the darkness that aśoka-tree; 
#### 51
तस्य स्कन्धे चिताधूमदग्धस्य क्रव्यगन्धिनः ।
सोऽपश्यल्लम्बमानं तं भूतस्येव शवं तरोः ।। ५१

the tree was scorched with the smoke of funeral pyres, and smelt of raw flesh, and looked like a Bhúta, and he saw the corpse hanging on its trunk, as it were on the shoulder of a demon. 
#### 52
आरुह्य चात्र भूमौ तं च्छिन्नरज्जुमपातयत् ।
पतितश्चात्र सोऽकस्माच्चक्रन्द व्यथितो यथा ।। ५२

So he climbed up, and cutting the string which held it, flung it to the ground. And the moment it was flung down, it cried out, as if in pain. 
#### 53
ततोऽवरुह्य कृपया जीवाशङ्की स तस्य यत् ।
राजाङ्गं प्रामृशत्तेन सोऽट्टहासं व्यधाच्छवः ।। ५३

Then the king, supposing it was alive, came down and rubbed its body out of compassion; that made the corpse utter a loud demoniac laugh. 
#### 54
ततः स राजा मत्त्वा तं वेतालाधिष्ठितं तदा ।
किं हसस्येहि गच्छाव इति यावदकम्पितः ।। ५४

Then the king knew that it was possessed by a Vetála, and said without flinching, “Why do you laugh? Come, let us go off.” 
#### 55
वक्ति तावन्न भूमौ सवेतालं शवमैक्षत ।
ऐक्षतात्रैव वृक्षे तु लम्बमानं स्थितं पुनः ।। ५५

And immediately he missed from the ground the corpse possessed by the Vetála, and perceived that it was once more suspended on that very tree. 
#### 56
ततोऽधिरुह्य भूयोऽपि तमवातारयत्ततः ।
वज्रादपि हि वीराणां चित्तरत्नमखण्डितम् ।। ५६

Then he climbed up again and brought it down, for the heart of heroes is a gem more impenetrable than adamant. 
#### 57
आरोप्य च सवेतालं स्कन्धे मौनेन तं शवम् ।
स त्रिविक्रमसेनोऽथ राजा गन्तुं प्रचक्रमे ।। ५७

Then king Trivikramasena threw the corpse possessed by a Vetála over his shoulder, and proceeded to go off with it, in silence. 
#### 58
यान्तं च तं शवान्तःस्थो वेतालोंऽसस्थितोऽब्रवीत् ।
राजन्नध्वविनोदाय कथामाख्यामि ते शृणु ।। ५८

And as he was going along, the Vetála in the corpse that was on his shoulder said to him, “King, I will tell you a story to beguile the way, listen.”
