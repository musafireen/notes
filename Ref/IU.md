## 1.1
https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/ishavasya-bhashya-by-sitarama/d/doc145018.html

ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।
तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्य स्विद्धनम् ॥ १ ॥

All this—whatsoever moveth on the earth—should he covered by the Lord. That renounced, enjoy. Covet not anybody’s wealth.

## 1.4
अनेजद् एकं मनसो जवीयो  
नै्नद् देवा आप्नुवन् पूर्वं अर्षत  
तद् धावतो ’न्यान् अति॒ति तिष्ठत  
तस्मिन्न् अपो मातरिश्वा दधाति

One mind alone is swift;  
the gods did not attain it previously.  
As it runs, it surpasses others and stands firm;  
in it the waters place the Mother’s strength.
## 1.5
तद् एजति तन् नैजति तद् दूरे तद् व अन्तिकेतद् अन्तर् अस्य सर्वस्यतद् उ सर्वस्यास्य बाह्यतः

It moves that, it does not move that, that is far away;  
that is near, that is within all, that is outside of all.
